① 模擬題目

隨著人工智慧與自動駕駛技術的發展，無人駕駛計程車（self-driving taxis）已開始在部分城市提供載客服務。假設未來你居住的城市也開始提供這項服務，請寫一篇英文作文，文分兩段。

第一段：說明你是否願意搭乘無人駕駛計程車，並說明理由。

第二段：說明你認為無人駕駛計程車普及後，可能為人們的生活或社會帶來哪些改變，並說明你的看法。

② 你會搭乘嗎？

先選立場，再想 2 個理由。

③ 理由素材卡

交通便利｜make transportation more convenient

They could still provide rides when few human drivers are available, especially late at night or during busy hours.

即使可提供服務的人類駕駛不多，尤其是在深夜或尖峰時段，無人駕駛計程車仍能提供載客服務。

交通安全｜reduce accidents caused by human error

For example, automated driving systems do not get tired or lose focus behind the wheel.

例如，自動駕駛系統不會因疲勞而在駕駛時失去專注力。

提升行動自主性｜travel more independently

Elderly people and people with limited mobility would no longer need to depend entirely on family members for transportation.

年長者與行動不便者將不必再完全依賴家人接送。

科技依賴｜greater dependence on self-driving systems

If the system suddenly failed, passengers might have difficulty dealing with the situation on their own.

如果系統突然故障，乘客可能難以自行處理當下的狀況。

駕駛工作｜impact on professional drivers

For instance, taxi drivers may have fewer job opportunities if more passengers choose driverless services.

例如，如果更多乘客選擇無人駕駛服務，計程車司機可能會面臨較少的工作機會。

⑧ 完整參考範文

查看完整範文

As technology continues to develop, self-driving taxis may soon become part of our daily lives. If this service became available in my city, I would be willing to give it a try. There are several reasons why I would choose this service. One reason is that self-driving taxis could make transportation more convenient. They could still provide rides when few human drivers are available, especially late at night or during busy hours. Another reason is that they may reduce accidents caused by human error. For example, automated driving systems do not get tired or lose focus behind the wheel.

The widespread use of self-driving taxis could change the way people travel. One important change is that elderly people and people with limited mobility may be able to travel more independently. They would no longer need to depend entirely on family members for transportation. However, greater dependence on self-driving systems may also cause problems. If the system suddenly failed, passengers might have difficulty dealing with the situation on their own. Another possible concern is the impact on professional drivers. For instance, taxi drivers may have fewer job opportunities if more passengers choose driverless services. In my opinion, self-driving taxis could bring many benefits, but safety should always come first.
